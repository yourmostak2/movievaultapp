Movie Vault

Movie Vault is a free Android app to keep track of the movies and web series you want to watch, are watching, and have watched. See new releases in theaters and on streaming, follow episodes season by season, and keep your favorites in one place.

Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Watchlist, Watched and Favorites lists
  • Season and episode tracking for series
  • Posters, ratings, cast and where-to-watch information
  • Hollywood, Bollywood, Tollywood and Dhallywood titles
  • Optional account and Google Drive backup so your lists are never lost
  • 9 languages, light and dark themes
